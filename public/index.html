    🌍 How to Play GeoGuess LIVE

    • Look at the shape on screen — it's the silhouette of a real country.
    • Type the country's name in the TikTok comments (e.g. France, japan) to guess.
    • Each guess shows how close you were: a % score, distance in km, and an arrow pointing toward the real country.
    • You get one guess per round — make it count!
    • First correct guess wins the round and earns points. Everyone climbs the Top 10 leaderboard as they play.
    • After 6 wrong guesses with no winner, the answer is revealed and a new round starts automatically.
